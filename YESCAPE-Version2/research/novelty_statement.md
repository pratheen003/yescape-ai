## Overview

YEScape 2.0 is proposed as an internship-specific AI verification framework rather than a conventional text-based scam detection system. Unlike existing approaches that primarily classify textual content, YEScape integrates multiple independently verifiable trust signals into a unified evidence-driven trust assessment pipeline.

The framework emphasizes transparent decision support by combining machine learning predictions with real-world verification evidence, enabling users to understand not only whether an internship appears trustworthy but also why that decision was reached.

---

# Core Research Novelty

The primary novelty of YEScape 2.0 lies in its methodology rather than the individual algorithms it employs.

Instead of relying solely on textual fraud classification, the proposed framework performs internship verification through supervised multi-signal trust fusion by integrating heterogeneous evidence sources into a single explainable trust assessment.

---

# Novel Contributions

## 1. Internship-Specific Verification Framework

The proposed framework is specifically designed for internship verification rather than generic employment fraud detection.

This enables the system to capture behavioural patterns and verification requirements unique to internship recruitment.

---

## 2. Multi-Signal Trust Fusion

YEScape combines five independent verification signals:

- Machine Learning Scam Probability
- Domain Intelligence
- Recruiter Identity Verification
- Company Verification
- Context Intelligence

Each signal contributes independently to the final trust assessment through a supervised fusion model.

---

## 3. Evidence-Driven Trust Assessment

Instead of producing only a binary prediction, the framework generates an explainable YESScore supported by transparent evidence from every verification signal.

This transforms fraud detection into an interpretable decision-support system.

---

## 4. Internship Benchmark Dataset

The research introduces a manually curated internship benchmark dataset designed specifically for evaluating internship verification systems.

This addresses the lack of internship-focused evaluation datasets currently available in the literature.

---

# Research Contribution

The proposed methodology contributes a unified internship verification framework that combines machine learning, independently verifiable trust evidence, supervised trust fusion, and explainable AI into a reproducible trust assessment pipeline.

Rather than proposing a new machine learning algorithm, YEScape contributes a novel verification methodology capable of supporting transparent and evidence-based internship legitimacy assessment.

---

# Summary

YEScape 2.0 advances internship verification by shifting from isolated text classification toward a comprehensive evidence-driven trust assessment framework.

Its novelty lies in integrating heterogeneous trust signals through supervised trust fusion while providing transparent reasoning that supports trustworthy AI-assisted decision making.
